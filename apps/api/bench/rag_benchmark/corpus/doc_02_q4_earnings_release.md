# Tarnwick Industrial Group Reports Fourth-Quarter and Full-Year Fiscal 2025 Results

**HALLOWMERE, 6 November 2025.** Tarnwick Industrial Group (the "Company") today reported results for the fourth quarter and the fiscal year ended 30 September 2025.

## Full-year overview

Sales for the twelve months reached $4,187.3 million, 1.7% above the prior year, with growth in two of the Company's three segments more than offsetting a softer result in the third. Adjusted EBITDA margin for the year was 14.6%. Net income attributable to shareholders came to $276.4 million, a figure that reflects the non-cash and restructuring items described in the accompanying notes to the accounts. The Company generated cash ahead of its internal plan and ended the year with comfortable liquidity and no borrowings under its revolving facility.

## Fourth-quarter commentary

Quarterly performance was held back by events at the Dunmarrow plant. A failure of the plant's coolant system forced the facility offline for six weeks during the period, and gross margin slipped to 27.9% from 29.4% in the same quarter a year earlier. Management attributes the shortfall almost entirely to that interruption; the underlying price-cost position elsewhere in the portfolio was stable to slightly favourable. Production at Dunmarrow returned to its normal rate before the quarter closed, and the Company has since commissioned an independent engineering review of the plant's cooling infrastructure.

Selling and administrative costs were well controlled in the quarter. Working capital improved sequentially as inventories were reduced and collections remained prompt, and the Company's cash conversion for the year exceeded its target range.

## Segment highlights

**Fluid Controls** delivered the strongest growth of the three segments, driven by replacement demand, selective price increases and a healthy order intake from utilities customers. Order backlog at year end was above the level a year earlier.

**Industrial Services** continued to convert its backlog and added several multi-year maintenance agreements, including two framework contracts with regional power operators. Utilisation of the field workforce improved through the year.

**Precision Components** saw lower volumes as customers worked down inventory early in the fiscal year, compounded by the production interruption described above. Quoting activity picked up in the final weeks of the year, and management expects a gradual recovery through fiscal 2026.

## Management comment

"This was a year in which the portfolio showed both its strengths and its weak points," said Ingrid Vasterholm, Chief Executive Officer. "Two of our three businesses grew and gained share. In the third, we took decisive action on footprint and we dealt with an operational failure that we have no intention of repeating. I am grateful to the teams who worked long hours to bring Dunmarrow back to full rate."

Odalys Brennmark, Chief Financial Officer, added: "Cash generation was better than we planned and our liquidity position is strong. We will continue to run the balance sheet conservatively while we invest in the parts of the business that offer the best returns."

## Outlook

For fiscal 2026, the Company expects organic sales growth in the low single digits and a modest recovery in gross margin as production at Dunmarrow normalises. The outlook does not include the effect of any transactions that have not yet closed. Capital spending priorities, including investment in replacement capacity, will be described separately.

## About Tarnwick Industrial Group

Tarnwick supplies precision-machined components, flow-control equipment and field maintenance services to customers in energy, water, transport and general industry. The Company operates in 14 countries.
