# Tarnwick Industrial Group
## Segment Reporting, Fiscal Year 2025

*Amounts in millions of US dollars. Segment figures are shown before intersegment eliminations, so they do not sum to consolidated totals.*

### Basis of presentation

The Company reports three operating segments, which correspond to the way the chief operating decision maker reviews performance and allocates resources: Precision Components, Fluid Controls and Industrial Services. Segment performance is evaluated on revenue and operating profit before corporate costs, restructuring charges and impairment charges.

### Segment discussion

**Precision Components.** This segment manufactures machined and cast components for transport, energy and general industrial customers. It operates the Dunmarrow plant, the group's largest single production site by headcount, along with five smaller machining facilities. Revenue fell during the year as a result of customer destocking in the first half and lost output in the fourth quarter. It was the only segment where revenue was lower than in the prior year. Operating margin was reduced by under-absorbed fixed costs. Management expects volumes to improve in fiscal 2026 as customer inventories normalise and as capacity is restored.

**Fluid Controls.** The segment designs and sells valves, actuators and metering equipment for water, gas and process applications. Revenue growth was broad-based across regions, with particular strength in North America and in the Gulf. Price realisation was positive and input cost pressures eased in the second half. The segment includes the Aquaflow Controls business, whose results were weighed down by weaker municipal demand during the year.

**Industrial Services.** The segment provides inspection, maintenance and repair services on customer sites, mostly under multi-year agreements. About two-thirds of its revenue is contracted and recurring. Growth in the year came from new contract wins and from higher utilisation of the field workforce, rather than from price. Margins are structurally lower than in the manufacturing segments but are less volatile, and cash conversion is high.

### Geographic information

Revenue is attributed to countries based on the location of the customer. North America remained the largest region, followed by Europe and the Middle East. No single customer accounted for more than 6% of consolidated revenue in either fiscal 2025 or fiscal 2024. Long-lived assets are concentrated in the United States, Germany and Poland.

### Capital employed

Capital employed is reviewed by management at the segment level, but it is not a measure of segment profit or loss and is not separately disclosed in these tables. The Precision Components segment is the most capital-intensive of the three, accounting for the majority of the Company's property, plant and equipment.
