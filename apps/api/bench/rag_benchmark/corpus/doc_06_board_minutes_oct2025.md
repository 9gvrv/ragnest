# Tarnwick Industrial Group plc
## Minutes of a Meeting of the Board of Directors

**Date:** 28 October 2025
**Location:** Registered office, Hallowmere (with two directors attending by video)
**Status:** Approved at the following meeting; confidential

**Present:** Alasdair Pennhallow (Chair), Ingrid Vasterholm (Chief Executive Officer), Odalys Brennmark (Executive Director and Chief Financial Officer), Priyanka Delacroix-Oyelaran, Thaddeus Okonjo-Reyes, Marguerite Szabo-Lindqvist, Henrik Abernathy-Cole.

**In attendance:** Ruben Achterberg (General Counsel and Company Secretary).

### 1. Apologies and declarations of interest

No apologies were received. The Chair asked whether any director wished to declare an interest in any item on the agenda. Ms Brennmark noted that she would raise a matter under item 5, and no other declarations were made.

### 2. Minutes of the previous meeting

The minutes of the meeting held in September were approved as a correct record and signed by the Chair. Matters arising were covered elsewhere on the agenda.

### 3. Health, safety and environment

The CEO presented the monthly safety report. There were no lost-time injuries during the month at the group's manufacturing sites. One near-miss involving a forklift at a European distribution centre had been investigated and corrective actions were in place. Mr Okonjo-Reyes asked about trends in contractor incidents; the CEO undertook to provide a breakdown at the next meeting.

### 4. Dunmarrow plant review

The Chief Operating Officer joined the meeting to update the Board on the investigation into the recent coolant system failure. The independent engineers had issued an interim finding that identified a maintenance planning weakness rather than a design fault. The Board discussed the adequacy of the plant's contingency planning and asked management to bring back a proposal for strengthening it. Ms Delacroix-Oyelaran asked that the Audit Committee receive a summary of the control lessons learned.

### 5. Proposed acquisition of Corvane Dynamics

The Chair introduced the item. Ms Brennmark reminded the Board that she served as Chief Financial Officer of Corvane Dynamics until 2022. She confirmed that she holds no financial interest in Corvane, but the General Counsel advised, and she agreed, that she should not take part in the discussion or the vote. She left the meeting for the duration of the item.

The CEO presented the strategic rationale, the diligence findings and the financing plan, as set out in the transaction paper circulated in advance. Directors asked about integration risk, customer overlap, and the regulatory timetable. The General Counsel advised that the transaction would require clearance from a competition authority and described the expected process. Mr Abernathy-Cole asked that the Board receive a post-signing integration plan within sixty days.

**Resolved:** that the Company enter into the transaction on the terms set out in the paper tabled at the meeting, and that the Chief Executive Officer be authorised to sign the definitive agreements and announce the transaction. The resolution was passed by the six directors participating, with no votes against. Ms Brennmark rejoined the meeting after the vote.

### 6. Audit Committee update

Ms Delacroix-Oyelaran, as Committee Chair, reported that the external audit of the fiscal year was progressing to plan and that no significant issues had been raised by the auditor. Year-end work on inventory valuation and goodwill was ongoing. She confirmed that the Committee would meet again before the publication of the results.

### 7. Corporate calendar

The Company Secretary tabled the proposed calendar for the next twelve months, including dates for committee meetings and the annual general meeting. The calendar was approved subject to minor changes.

### 8. Any other business

The Chair thanked the CEO and the operations team for the pace of the response at Dunmarrow. There being no further business, the meeting closed at 12:35.

*Signed as a correct record: ____________________ Chair*
