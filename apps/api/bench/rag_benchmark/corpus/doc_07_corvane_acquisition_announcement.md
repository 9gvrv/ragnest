# Tarnwick Industrial Group to Acquire Corvane Dynamics

**HALLOWMERE, 17 November 2025.** Tarnwick Industrial Group ("Tarnwick") today announced that it has entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Corvane Dynamics ("Corvane"), a manufacturer of high-pressure valve actuators and associated control electronics.

## Transaction summary

Tarnwick will pay $734 million for Corvane, with consideration made up of 60% cash and the remainder in newly issued Tarnwick shares. The cash portion will be funded from existing resources and available facilities. Closing is expected in the second quarter of Tarnwick's 2026 fiscal year, subject to approval from the Eastmark Competition Authority and the satisfaction of customary conditions. Shares issued to Corvane's selling shareholders will be subject to a twelve-month lock-up.

## About Corvane

Corvane was founded in 1987 and supplies actuators to oil and gas, chemicals and power generation customers. It employs about 1,900 people at three plants and two engineering centres, and had annual revenue of approximately $388 million in its most recent fiscal year. Its products are specified into long-lived installations, and a large share of its revenue comes from aftermarket parts and service.

## Strategic rationale

Tarnwick believes the combination is a natural fit with its Fluid Controls segment. Corvane's actuators complement Tarnwick's valves, and the two companies already supply many of the same end customers, often through different purchasing channels. The combination is expected to allow:

- a broader product offering for process-industry customers,
- shared investment in sensing and diagnostics technology,
- cost efficiencies in procurement and logistics over time.

Tarnwick expects the transaction to be accretive to earnings per share in the second full year after closing, excluding one-time integration costs. The Company does not expect the transaction to alter its capital allocation priorities or its dividend policy.

## Leadership and integration

Corvane will operate as part of the Fluid Controls segment. Lucan Marchetti-Voss, Corvane's Chief Executive Officer, is expected to remain with the business through the integration period. Tarnwick has established an integration steering group, which will report to the Board, and intends to retain Corvane's brand for products sold under existing specifications.

## Comments

"Corvane is a respected name with a loyal customer base and strong engineering," said Ingrid Vasterholm, Tarnwick's Chief Executive Officer. "We have known the company for years, and we believe this is a fair outcome for both sets of shareholders."

"This partnership gives our team the scale and resources to do more for our customers," said Lucan Marchetti-Voss.

## Advisers

Tarnwick was advised on the transaction by independent financial and legal advisers. Corvane was advised by its own advisers.

## About Tarnwick Industrial Group

Tarnwick supplies precision-machined components, flow-control equipment and field maintenance services to customers in energy, water, transport and general industry.
