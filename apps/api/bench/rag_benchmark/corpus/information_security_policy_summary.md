# Tarnwick Industrial Group
## Information Security Policy (Summary for Employees)

### Purpose

This summary explains the basic rules that every employee and contractor must follow to protect Tarnwick's information and systems. The full policy and supporting standards are available on the intranet. Information security is a shared responsibility, and everyone has a role to play.

### Passwords and access

Use a unique password for every system, and use the approved password manager to store them. Multi-factor authentication is required for remote access and for all administrative accounts. Access to systems is granted according to job role and reviewed regularly; if you change roles, tell your manager so that your access can be updated. Never share your credentials with colleagues, including IT support staff.

### Email and phishing

Be cautious with unexpected messages that ask you to click a link, open an attachment or make an urgent payment. Report suspicious messages using the button in your email client. The security team runs simulated phishing exercises throughout the year, and the results are used to improve training rather than to penalise individuals. If you believe you have clicked a malicious link, report it immediately; early action limits harm.

### Devices and data

Company laptops and phones must be encrypted and kept up to date. Do not connect personal storage devices to company computers or plant-floor systems. Confidential information should be stored only in approved locations and should not be sent to personal email accounts. When travelling, keep devices with you, and use the company VPN on public networks.

### Industrial control systems

Systems that run machinery and processes are segmented from the corporate network. Changes to these systems must go through change control, and remote access is provided only through managed gateways. Vendors that need access to equipment must be approved in advance and supervised.

### Use of artificial intelligence tools

Employees may use only approved artificial intelligence tools for work purposes. Do not paste confidential, customer or personal information into tools that are not approved by the security team. Outputs from these tools should be reviewed by a person before being used in customer communications or engineering decisions.

### Incident reporting

Report any actual or suspected security incident to the service desk without delay, at any time of day. Do not attempt to investigate or fix it yourself, and do not delete evidence. The incident response team will coordinate containment and communications.

### Consequences

Failure to follow this policy may lead to disciplinary action, in accordance with local law. Questions about the policy should be directed to the information security team.
