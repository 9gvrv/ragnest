# Tarnwick Industrial Group
## Investor Relations: Calendar and Contacts

### Corporate calendar (indicative)

The Company expects to publish information on or around the dates below. Dates may change, and the Company will announce any change through its usual channels.

| Event | Expected timing |
|---|---|
| Publication of annual report | Late in the calendar year |
| Annual general meeting | 24 February 2026 |
| Half-year results | Spring 2026 |
| Interim management statements | As required |

### Results communications

Results announcements are released through a regulatory news service and then published on the Investor Relations website. For each results announcement, management hosts a live conference call and webcast. Participants may submit questions by telephone or in writing. Recordings and transcripts remain available on the website for at least twelve months.

### Quiet periods

The Company observes a quiet period before the announcement of results. During this period, members of management do not meet with investors or analysts to discuss current trading. Requests for meetings received during the quiet period are handled after the announcement.

### Meeting requests

Investors who would like to meet the Company can contact the Investor Relations team. The team arranges meetings with management, plant visits for investors, and participation in conferences. Visits to manufacturing sites are scheduled well in advance, and visitors will be asked to complete a safety induction before access to production areas.

### Shareholder services

Questions about shareholdings, share certificates, changes of address and dividend payment instructions should be directed to the Company's registrar. Shareholders can manage their holdings online and elect to receive communications electronically. Beware of unsolicited telephone calls offering to buy or sell shares; the Company does not endorse any such services.
