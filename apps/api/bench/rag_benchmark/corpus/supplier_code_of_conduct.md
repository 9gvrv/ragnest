# Tarnwick Industrial Group
## Supplier Code of Conduct (Summary)

*Applies to all suppliers, contractors and their subcontractors.*

### Why we have a code

Our customers rely on us to deliver safe, high-quality products on time, and we rely on our suppliers to help us do so. This code explains what we expect from the companies we buy from. It is a condition of doing business with Tarnwick, and compliance may be verified through questionnaires, audits and site visits.

### Legal compliance and ethics

Suppliers must comply with all laws that apply to them, including those on anti-bribery, competition, trade controls, data protection and the environment. Suppliers must not offer or accept bribes, facilitation payments or inappropriate gifts, and must keep accurate records of transactions with Tarnwick. Conflicts of interest involving Tarnwick employees must be disclosed to our procurement team in writing.

### Labour and human rights

Suppliers must not use forced, bonded or child labour. Workers should be free to leave their employment with reasonable notice, should be paid at least the legal minimum wage, and should not be asked to work hours beyond those permitted by law. Suppliers must provide a safe and healthy working environment, including appropriate protective equipment, emergency procedures and access to clean facilities. We expect suppliers to respect workers' rights to associate freely and to bargain collectively where the law allows.

### Environment

Suppliers should manage their environmental impact responsibly, including emissions, waste and the use of hazardous substances. Where our products are subject to substance restrictions, suppliers must provide the declarations and test results we request. We encourage suppliers to set their own reduction goals and to share information on progress.

### Responsible sourcing of minerals

Suppliers of metals and electronic components are expected to carry out due diligence on the origin of materials, in line with recognised international guidance, and to cooperate with our requests for information. We may ask suppliers to identify the smelters and refiners in their supply chains.

### Quality and traceability

Suppliers must maintain a documented quality system and must notify us promptly of any nonconformity that could affect product already delivered. Materials certificates must be retained for the period specified in the purchase terms. Changes to processes, materials or manufacturing locations that could affect form, fit or function require our prior approval.

### Reporting concerns

Anyone who has a concern about a possible breach of this code can report it to Tarnwick's confidential reporting line, which is available in multiple languages. Tarnwick does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

### Consequences of non-compliance

A breach of this code may lead to corrective action requirements, suspension of orders or termination of the relationship. We prefer to work with suppliers to fix problems where that is practical, but we will act promptly in cases of serious violation.
