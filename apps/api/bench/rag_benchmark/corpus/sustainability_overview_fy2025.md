# Tarnwick Industrial Group
## Sustainability Overview, Fiscal Year 2025

### Our approach

Sustainability at Tarnwick is part of how we run our plants and serve our customers, not a separate programme. Our approach focuses on three themes that matter most to the business: using energy and materials efficiently, keeping people safe, and being a dependable neighbour in the communities where we operate. The Executive Leadership Team reviews progress each quarter, and the Board's Risk and Sustainability Committee oversees the long-term strategy.

### Energy and emissions

Most of our direct emissions come from heat-treatment furnaces, casting operations and the vehicles used by our field service teams. We have set a goal to reduce Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions by 30% by 2030 from a 2020 baseline, and we report progress against it each year. During the year we continued our programme of furnace upgrades, replaced a number of older compressors with variable-speed units, and signed power purchase agreements for renewable electricity at two European sites. Waste heat recovery is now in use at several machining facilities, where it is used to warm adjacent buildings.

Energy audits were conducted at every manufacturing site. The audits identified a range of low-cost measures, such as improved insulation, compressed-air leak repair and lighting upgrades, which site managers are now implementing. We expect most of these to pay back within two years.

### Materials and waste

Metal is our largest material input, so scrap reduction is both an environmental and a commercial priority. Recycled content in our castings continues to be high, and we are trialling new coolant formulations that last longer and generate less waste. Several sites send machining swarf to specialist recyclers rather than disposing of it. We are also working with suppliers to reduce packaging, including a pilot with reusable crates for deliveries between our own plants.

### Water

Our flow-control products help customers conserve water, and we try to apply the same discipline internally. Sites in water-stressed regions have installed closed-loop cooling and rainwater collection systems. We monitor withdrawal and discharge quality at all sites and report any exceedance to the relevant regulator.

### People and communities

We believe a safe and inclusive workplace is a prerequisite for good performance. Our apprenticeship programmes operate in several countries, and graduates of the programmes now fill a growing share of skilled technician roles. We encourage colleagues to volunteer, and each site has a small community fund that is directed by local employees. Examples this year included equipment donations to a technical school, support for a local food bank, and a river clean-up organised by a plant's safety committee.

### Governance and reporting

Sustainability data is reviewed by internal audit on a rotating basis, and selected metrics are subject to external assurance. We publish more detail in our annual sustainability data supplement, which is available on the Company's website. We welcome feedback from customers, investors and other stakeholders on how we can improve the clarity and usefulness of our reporting.
